📚 Fertilizer Details & Information

🌾 Nitrogen (N)

Nitrogen is essential for plant growth and chlorophyll production. It promotes:

  • Leaf and stem growth
  • Protein synthesis
  • Vegetative growth
  • Deep green leaf color
  • Increased crop yield

🌻 Phosphorous (P)

Phosphorous supports energy transfer and root development:

  • Root system strengthening
  • Flower and fruit production
  • Energy metabolism
  • Disease resistance
  • Seed development

💪 Potassium (K)

Potassium regulates plant functions and enhances quality:

  • Water retention
  • Disease resistance
  • Fruit quality improvement
  • Stress tolerance
  • Plant vigor

⚖️ NPK Balance

The NPK ratio is crucial for optimal plant nutrition:

  • Different crops need different ratios
  • Balanced nutrients prevent deficiencies
  • Soil testing determines exact needs
  • Seasonal adjustments are important
  • Overuse can harm the soil

🌍 Soil Types

Different soils have different nutrient-holding capacities:

  • Loamy: Best for most crops
  • Black: Rich in organic matter
  • Clayey: Compact, needs drainage
  • Red: Acidic, needs amendments
  • Sandy: Drains quickly, needs frequent feeding

🌡️ Environmental Factors

Temperature and humidity affect nutrient availability:

  • Temperature impacts nutrient uptake
  • Humidity affects disease development
  • Moisture influences nutrient availability
  • Seasonal changes require adjustments
  • Weather patterns guide application timing
